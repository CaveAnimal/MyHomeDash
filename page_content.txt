        <h1>Irrigation Project Goals</h1>
<h2>Project Statement</h2>
<p>Build a partially removable irrigation system where most of the plumbing runs and the protective shielding for wiring are made from old hoses cut to fit. The hose-based paths stay in place over winter, while the sprinkler heads and switches are removed seasonally.</p>
<h2>Core Goals</h2>
<ul>
<li>Use old hoses as the main material for both water routing and protective shielding for irrigation wiring.</li>
<li>Keep the hoses installed over winter so the permanent layout does not need to be rebuilt each season.</li>
<li>Make the sprinkler heads removable.</li>
<li>Make the switches removable.</li>
<li>Ensure each switch is wired correctly.</li>
<li>Test each sprinkler head individually before considering it complete.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Materials And Reference Pictures</h2>
<h3>Main Controller Candidate</h3>
<figure class="mhd-markdown-figure"><img src="https://static-data2.manualslib.com/product-images/09a/1401601/300x300/orbit-b-hyve.jpg" alt="Orbit B-hyve 12-station controller model 57950" class="mhd-markdown-image rounded border">
<figcaption>Orbit B-hyve 12-station controller model 57950</figcaption>
</figure>
<ul>
<li>Controller target: Orbit B-hyve 12-station indoor/outdoor smart irrigation controller</li>
<li>Model number: 57950</li>
<li>Role: main controller for the first prototype and the larger system build-out</li>
<li>Source reference: <a href="https://www.manualslib.com/manual/1401601/Orbit-B-Hyve.html#product-B-hyve%2057950" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Orbit B-hyve 57950 manual listing</a></li>
<li>Status: need to confirm whether this controller is already on hand or needs to be purchased</li>
</ul>
<h2>What I Have Or Expect To Reuse</h2>
<ul>
<li>Old hoses to cut and fit for the main water runs.</li>
<li>Old hoses to use as protective shielding over exposed wiring runs where practical.</li>
<li>A design approach that leaves the hose-based path in place through winter.</li>
</ul>
<h2>What I Still Need To Confirm Or Acquire</h2>
<ul>
<li>The main controller, starting with Orbit 57950.</li>
<li>The removable sprinkler heads for each active zone.</li>
<li>The removable switches and the full switch count.</li>
<li>Wiring, waterproof connectors, and mounting hardware needed to wire each switch correctly.</li>
<li>Any additional fittings, adapters, or clamps needed to mate the hose-based runs to the controller and sprinkler heads.</li>
</ul>
<h2>System Intent</h2>
<p>The system should be easy to partially disassemble at the end of the season without tearing out the full layout. The hoses act as the long-term physical path, while the more exposed or fragile parts, especially heads and switches, are the parts to remove and reinstall.</p>
<h2>Prototype Goal For This Weekend</h2>
<ul>
<li>Get the first irrigation zone or first unit working this weekend.</li>
<li>Install the first hose-based run in its intended layout.</li>
<li>Wire the first switch.</li>
<li>Connect and test the first sprinkler head.</li>
<li>Confirm that the switch activates correctly and the head operates as expected.</li>
<li>Confirm that the removable parts can be detached without disturbing the hose path that remains in place.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Work Requirements</h2>
<ul>
<li>Each switch must be wired before it can be tested.</li>
<li>Each sprinkler head must be tested for operation, placement, and basic reliability.</li>
<li>The prototype should prove that the hose-based approach works for both water routing and wire protection.</li>
<li>The prototype should also confirm that seasonal removal of heads and switches is practical.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Initial Success Criteria</h2>
<ul>
<li>The first prototype unit runs successfully this weekend.</li>
<li>The first switch activates the intended sprinkler head.</li>
<li>The first head delivers water correctly without obvious leaks or misrouting.</li>
<li>The hose remains in place as the reusable base structure.</li>
<li>The switch and sprinkler head can be removed after testing without dismantling the full run.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Estimated Completion Approach</h2>
<ul>
<li>This weekend: complete the first working prototype.</li>
<li>Next 1 to 2 weekends: wire remaining switches and test additional heads.</li>
<li>Following 1 to 3 weekends: refine fit, durability, removability, and seasonal handling.</li>
<li>Overall first usable system estimate: 3 to 6 weekends depending on the number of heads, switches, and adjustments needed after the prototype.</li>
</ul>

      <div class="card-body mhd-project-doc">
        <h1>Irrigation Decisions</h1>
<h2>DECISION-001</h2>
<h3>Decision</h3>
<p>Use old hoses as the main seasonal backbone for both water routing and protective shielding over wiring where practical.</p>
<h3>Why</h3>
<ul>
<li>The hose material can be cut to fit the layout.</li>
<li>Leaving the hose runs in place over winter reduces rebuild work.</li>
<li>It supports the goal of a partially removable system instead of a fully rebuilt one.</li>
</ul>
<h2>DECISION-002</h2>
<h3>Decision</h3>
<p>Remove sprinkler heads and switches seasonally while leaving the hose-based path in place.</p>
<h3>Why</h3>
<ul>
<li>The heads and switches are the parts most likely to need protection, replacement, or easier off-season handling.</li>
<li>Seasonal removal is the key maintenance behavior the prototype needs to prove.</li>
</ul>
<h2>DECISION-003</h2>
<h3>Decision</h3>
<p>Start controller research and prototype planning with the Orbit B-hyve 12-station controller, model 57950.</p>
<h3>Why</h3>
<ul>
<li>It is the first named controller target for this project.</li>
<li>It gives the planning documents and prototype work a concrete controller reference.</li>
<li>The available manual provides immediate box-content and wiring references.</li>
</ul>
<h2>DECISION-004</h2>
<h3>Decision</h3>
<p>Treat this weekend&#x27;s target as one working prototype path rather than a full system rollout.</p>
<h3>Why</h3>
<ul>
<li>A single successful controller-switch-head path is the fastest proof of concept.</li>
<li>It reduces risk before committing to the remaining zones.</li>
<li>It creates a realistic weekend-sized milestone.</li>
</ul>
