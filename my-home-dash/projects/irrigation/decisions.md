# Irrigation Decisions

## DECISION-001
### Decision
Use old hoses as the main seasonal backbone for both water routing and protective shielding over wiring where practical.

### Why
- The hose material can be cut to fit the layout.
- Leaving the hose runs in place over winter reduces rebuild work.
- It supports the goal of a partially removable system instead of a fully rebuilt one.

## DECISION-002
### Decision
Remove sprinkler heads and switches seasonally while leaving the hose-based path in place.

### Why
- The heads and switches are the parts most likely to need protection, replacement, or easier off-season handling.
- Seasonal removal is the key maintenance behavior the prototype needs to prove.

## DECISION-003
### Decision
Start controller research and prototype planning with the Orbit B-hyve 12-station controller, model 57950.

### Why
- It is the first named controller target for this project.
- It gives the planning documents and prototype work a concrete controller reference.
- The available manual provides immediate box-content and wiring references.

## DECISION-004
### Decision
Treat this weekend's target as one working prototype path rather than a full system rollout.

### Why
- A single successful controller-switch-head path is the fastest proof of concept.
- It reduces risk before committing to the remaining zones.
- It creates a realistic weekend-sized milestone.
