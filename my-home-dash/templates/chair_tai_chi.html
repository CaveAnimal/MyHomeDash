Free chair tai chi.

Gentle, low-impact movement you can do seated: balance, flexibility, and calm in 10-30 minutes a day.

Back to Home
Check with your doctor before starting, especially with balance problems, recent surgery, or heart conditions. Move within a pain-free range; stop if you feel dizzy or sharp pain.

Setup

  • Sturdy chair without arms and without wheels, on a non-slip floor.
  • Sit tall near the front edge, feet flat and hip-width apart, knees over ankles.
  • Shoulders relaxed, chin slightly tucked, as if a string lifts the crown of your head.
  • Breathe through the nose: slow in, slower out. Move at the speed of your breath.
  • Keep the knees soft and the movements smooth, never forced.

Core seated movements

  1. Commencement: raise both arms to shoulder height, then lower with a breath out.
  2. Holding the ball: hands circle in front of the chest as if holding a large ball.
  3. Wave hands like clouds: shift weight slightly side to side, hands drifting across the body.
  4. Parting the wild horse's mane: one hand rises, one presses down, alternating sides.
  5. Brush knee: sweep one hand past the knee while the other pushes forward.
  6. Lift the sky: interlace fingers, palms up overhead, stretch tall.
  7. Closing: hands rest on thighs, eyes closed for three slow breaths.
