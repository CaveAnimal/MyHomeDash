# Manage Project Goal

## Project Statement
Create and maintain a structured planning workspace for the Manage project so the work can be defined clearly, resourced properly, scheduled realistically, and completed in an organized way.

## Core Goals
- Provide a clear statement of the project and what successful completion looks like.
- Identify and gather the resources needed to do the work well.
- Plan and schedule the work with a weekend-first approach.
- Account for social commitments, household chores, and other real-life constraints before locking in work sessions.
- Provide reasonable completion estimates that can be revised as scope becomes clearer.

## Resources To Identify And Gather
- Project documents, notes, and reference materials.
- Required software, tools, accounts, and access.
- Time blocks available on weekends.
- A list of external dependencies, blockers, or people who may need to provide input.
- A simple tracking method for decisions, tasks, and progress.

## Scheduling Approach
- Prefer focused work blocks on weekends.
- Review the upcoming weekend in advance and check for appointments, family or social plans, errands, and household chores.
- Break work into smaller tasks that can fit into limited time windows.
- Keep at least one buffer block open for overruns, interruptions, or recovery time.
- Reassess the schedule weekly and update priorities based on progress and real availability.

## Estimated Completion
- Initial planning and resource gathering: 1 to 2 weekends.
- Detailed task breakdown and scheduling baseline: 1 weekend.
- Early execution and adjustment period: 2 to 4 weekends.
- Total initial project organization and launch estimate: 4 to 7 weekends.

## Completion Standard
The project is considered properly planned when the scope is defined, needed resources are identified, a realistic weekend-based schedule exists, major conflicts are accounted for, and completion estimates are documented and reviewable.
