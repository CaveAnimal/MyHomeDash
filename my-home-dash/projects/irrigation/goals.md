# Irrigation Project Goals

## Project Statement
Build a partially removable irrigation system where most of the plumbing runs and the protective shielding for wiring are made from old hoses cut to fit. The hose-based paths stay in place over winter, while the sprinkler heads and switches are removed seasonally.

## Core Goals
- Use old hoses as the main material for both water routing and protective shielding for irrigation wiring.
- Keep the hoses installed over winter so the permanent layout does not need to be rebuilt each season.
- Make the sprinkler heads removable.
- Make the switches removable.
- Ensure each switch is wired correctly.
- Test each sprinkler head individually before considering it complete.

## System Intent
The system should be easy to partially disassemble at the end of the season without tearing out the full layout. The hoses act as the long-term physical path, while the more exposed or fragile parts, especially heads and switches, are the parts to remove and reinstall.

## Prototype Goal For This Weekend
- Get the first irrigation zone or first unit working this weekend.
- Install the first hose-based run in its intended layout.
- Wire the first switch.
- Connect and test the first sprinkler head.
- Confirm that the switch activates correctly and the head operates as expected.
- Confirm that the removable parts can be detached without disturbing the hose path that remains in place.

## Work Requirements
- Each switch must be wired before it can be tested.
- Each sprinkler head must be tested for operation, placement, and basic reliability.
- The prototype should prove that the hose-based approach works for both water routing and wire protection.
- The prototype should also confirm that seasonal removal of heads and switches is practical.

## Initial Success Criteria
- The first prototype unit runs successfully this weekend.
- The first switch activates the intended sprinkler head.
- The first head delivers water correctly without obvious leaks or misrouting.
- The hose remains in place as the reusable base structure.
- The switch and sprinkler head can be removed after testing without dismantling the full run.

## Estimated Completion Approach
- This weekend: complete the first working prototype.
- Next 1 to 2 weekends: wire remaining switches and test additional heads.
- Following 1 to 3 weekends: refine fit, durability, removability, and seasonal handling.
- Overall first usable system estimate: 3 to 6 weekends depending on the number of heads, switches, and adjustments needed after the prototype.
